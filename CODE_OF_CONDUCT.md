# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/):
be respectful, assume good faith, and keep disagreements about the work,
not the person.

In short:

- Be welcoming to newcomers and differing viewpoints.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.

Reports of unacceptable behavior can be sent privately to the maintainer
(see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for a private contact path). Reports will
be handled with discretion.
